About

Clearer thinking makes better products

I help teams make sense of complex systems. That often means stepping back from screens to clarify the objects, language, workflows, and trust boundaries underneath—then carrying that structure through to a thoughtful product experience.

My practice sits between design, product, engineering, and knowledge architecture. I use research, systems thinking, and executable prototypes to turn ambiguity into shared understanding and useful, well-crafted software.
