About Me

I'm Andy, a designer who loves turning fuzzy ideas into clear, beautiful things people enjoy using. My work sits at the intersection of brand, product, and craft — I care as much about how something feels as how it looks.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me sketching, hunting for good type, or exploring a new coffee shop for inspiration.
